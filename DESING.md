## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the high-stakes, adrenaline-charged atmosphere of premier tier-1 competitive esports, drawing direct inspiration from Valve’s flagship competitive arenas: Dota 2's mystical, brutal Dire vs. Radiant clash and Counter-Strike 2's razor-sharp, tactical precision.

The aesthetic marries dark futuristic brutalism with refined dark-mode glassmorphism and the systematic ergonomics of modern developer/gaming tooling. Designed for competitive players, tournament organizers, analysts, and passionate spectators, the interface prioritizes hyper-focused data density, tactical scanability, and visceral energy.

Visual anchors include deep obsidian and midnight navy backdrops layered with translucent HUD surfaces, micro-textured borders, and razor-sharp geometric badges. Dynamic neon accents slice through the dark canvas: glowing electric crimson (radiant combat), tactical amber-gold (championship rewards, round alerts, economy counters), and cybernetic violet/cyan (utility states, active bracket lines). The resulting emotional tone is competitive, elite, uncompromising, and commanding.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for zero-fatigue high-contrast dark mode environments, anchoring content on a deeply layered obsidian canvas.

- **Primary (`#f43f5e`) - Radiant Crimson:** Used for high-priority actions, elimination notices, live tournament broadcasts, kill-feed highlights, and critical CTA states.
- **Secondary (`#6366f1`) - Tactical Violet/Indigo:** Governs utility elements, active bracket pathways, navigational focus states, platform telemetry, and system stats.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b`) - Combat Amber:** Designated for prize pool tallies, victory ranks, currency, MVP honors, and match countdown timers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0a0d14` to `#151b2b`):** Multi-tier surface system. Canvas base establishes the deep void; surface deep forms primary panels, sidebars, and structural HUD bays; surface card handles interactive cards and tournament grid tiles.

All surfaces utilize thin 1px frosted borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) to establish boundary clarity without heavy cognitive load. Glowing accent states are strictly functional, applied during hover, active bracket progression, or live status indications.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements three distinct, specialized font families:

1. **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines):** Delivers aggressive, futuristic angularity and bold presence suitable for championship titles, team match-up headers, and major hero banners.
2. **Geist (Body & Structural UI):** Provides neutral, clean, and hyper-legible text flow for tournament rulebooks, player descriptions, articles, and settings.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Data, HUD, & Tactical Labels):** Reserved for technical esports telemetry: match timers, live kill-death-assist (KDA) metrics, prize pool figures, seed brackets, and match IDs.

All uppercase badges leverage letter spacing ranging from `+0.06em` to `+0.08em` to reinforce an authenticated military/tactical HUD appearance.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a modular, fluid 12-column grid anchored by an asymmetrical shell: a compressed tactical utility sidebar (collapsible on tablet/mobile), a persistent global HUD top navigation with platform telemetry, and a central command stage.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid with 24px gutters and 40px outer margins. Tournament bracket structures dynamically overflow on a horizontal pan-and-zoom infinite stage while standard views (leaderboards, team dashboards) adhere tightly to the 12-column container.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters. Tactical navigation collapses into an expandable rail; bracket nodes compress into accordion-based match groupings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px margins. Cards stack vertically, metrics convert to side-by-side split badges, and hero match-ups shift to vertical face-offs.

Component padding adheres strictly to a baseline 4px/8px rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px), maintaining tight, tactical compactness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy diffuse drop shadows; instead, it utilizes a combination of tonal layering, glassmorphism, and neon luminous edge diffraction:

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Pure dark canvas (`#0a0d14`) with subtle ambient radial gradients (electric crimson at 5% opacity in top corners, indigo ambient low glows).
- **HUD Panels & Sidebars (Level 1):** Solid deep surface (`#0f1420`) with 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Cards & Floating Containers (Level 2):** Translucent backdrop-filtered surfaces (`rgba(21, 27, 43, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Bound by razor 1px borders.
- **Active / Interactive Hover (Level 3):** Cards elevate visually via localized neon perimeter glows (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px -3px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.25)` or `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`) accompanied by subtle border color brightness boosts.
- **Overlays, Tooltips, & Dialogs (Level 4):** Deep obsidian glass (`rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.9)`) combined with a fine dual border: outer `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` and inner hairline inset `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a restrained **Soft (`1`)** roundedness scale (standard radius: 4px; large: 8px; maximum: 12px) to communicate sharp tactical precision. Pill and circular shapes are restricted strictly to player avatar orbs and micro status dots.

Angled bevels and chamfered accents (45-degree micro cuts on active tabs, tournament badge corners, and CTA edges) are utilized to evoke Valve's distinctive hardware and tournament HUD visual language.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Tactical Radiant):** Solid `#f43f5e` fill, `#ffffff` text in `Space Grotesk 14px SemiBold`, uppercase. On hover: shifts to `#fb7185` with an outer crimson luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / HUD Button:** Dark glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), `#f8fafc` text. Hover brings a border tint of `#6366f1` and crisp inner luminescence.
- **Danger / Eliminator:** Outline `#ef4444` with 10% red background tint.

### Badges & Chips
- **Live Status:** Capsule with a pulsing neon red indicator dot, JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase, background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`, text `#fca5a5`.
- **Tournament Tier / Platform Chip:** Chamfered rectangular badge, dark obsidian background, 1px border colored by tier (Gold/Amber for Tier 1 Major, Cyan for Qualifier).

### Cards & Tournament Tiles
- Semi-transparent glass background (`#151b2b` at 75% opacity) over deep canvas.
- Integrated header zone featuring game iconography (Dota 2 Aegis icon, CS2 bomb/defusal glyphs), live countdown in JetBrains Mono, and prize pool highlight in Combat Amber (`#f59e0b`).
- Micro 1px gradient borders that subtly highlight from top-left to bottom-right.

### Tournament Bracket Tree Nodes
- Dual-team horizontal cell blocks connected by SVG vector bus lines.
- Team rows display seed index, team crest avatar (24x24px), mono score counters, and win/loss highlighting. Active/advancing teams illuminate with an indigo border glow and bold white typography; eliminated teams drop to `#64748b` opacity.

### Prize Pool Counters
- High-visibility tactical displays utilizing `Space Grotesk` and `JetBrains Mono`. Background consists of a deep obsidian container accented by an ambient golden gradient flare. The currency symbol is highlighted in luminous `#f59e0b`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Dark recessed background (`#0a0d14`), 1px muted border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- Font: `Geist 14px` for input values, `JetBrains Mono 11px` for floating label tags.
- Focus state triggers an immediate crisp border color change to `#6366f1` with zero fuzzy offset blur, accompanied by an ultra-thin 1px inset ring.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkboxes are 16x16px squares with 2px corner radius. Unchecked: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` border on deep background. Checked: `#f43f5e` fill with an angled white checkmark.
- Toggles feature a rectangular tactical slider container with a glowing thumb pip switching between `#64748b` (off) and `#6366f1` (on).
